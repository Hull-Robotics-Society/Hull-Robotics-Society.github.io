## Who are we?

### Welcome to the Hull University Robotics Society!

We are the Hull University Robotics Society, a community of passionate and innovative students dedicated to advancing the exciting field of robotics and automation. Our society brings together students from various disciplines to work on projects, develop skills, and learn about robotics in a fun and collaborative environment.

At Hull University, we believe in the power of robotics to change the world, and we are committed to fostering a space where members can explore the endless possibilities of this rapidly growing field. Whether you're a first-year student with a budding interest in robotics or an experienced engineer, we welcome you to join our community.

### Our Mission

The mission of the Hull University Robotics Society is to:

  - Foster Collaboration: We strive to create a collaborative environment where students can come together to work on exciting robotics projects. We encourage teamwork, knowledge-sharing, and the exchange of ideas.

  - Encourage Innovation: Our members are driven by the desire to innovate and push the boundaries of what robotics can achieve. Whether it's building robots for competitions or researching new technologies, we aim to bring fresh ideas to life.

## What We Do
### Projects & Competitions

At the Hull University Robotics Society, we love to put theory into practice. Our members have the opportunity to work on a variety of exciting robotics projects, from autonomous robots to drones, and even humanoid robots. We regularly participate in national and international robotics competitions, where we put our skills to the test against other top universities.
Workshops & Training

We offer a wide range of hands-on workshops designed to help our members improve their robotics skills. Whether you’re learning how to design circuits, program microcontrollers, or build robotic structures, our workshops are designed to cater to all skill levels. Our workshops help you develop the practical skills needed for success in the robotics field.

### Socials & Team-building

In addition to our technical activities, we believe in the importance of building friendships and a strong sense of community. We host regular social events, team-building activities, and fun challenges that allow our members to relax, have fun, and connect with like-minded individuals.

### Why Join Us?

Being a part of the Hull University Robotics Society provides you with a wealth of opportunities. Here’s why you should join:

  - Gain hands-on experience working on real-world robotics projects.
  - Collaborate with students from various backgrounds and disciplines, broadening your perspective.
  - Be part of a passionate and supportive community that shares your enthusiasm for robotics.

No prior experience with robotics? No problem! We welcome people of all levels, from complete beginners to advanced robotics enthusiasts.

### Our Location

We are based at the University of Hull, located in the heart of the vibrant city of Hull in the UK. Our society is open to all students from the University, regardless of your course or department. Whether you’re studying engineering, computer science, or any other subject, we encourage you to get involved and bring your unique perspective to the world of robotics!
