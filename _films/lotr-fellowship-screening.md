## 🎬 Movie Details

**Title**: The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (Extended Edition)

**Synopsis**:  
The peaceful life of the Shire is changed forever when Frodo Baggins inherits a mysterious ring with immense and terrible power. Joined by a fellowship of heroes, hobbits, men, an elf, a dwarf, and a wizard; Frodo sets out on a perilous quest to destroy the One Ring and save Middle-earth from the rising darkness of Sauron.  
The **Extended Edition** includes additional scenes that enrich the story, deepen character arcs, and expand Tolkien’s world even further.

**Cast**:  
- Elijah Wood as Frodo Baggins  
- Ian McKellen as Gandalf  
- Viggo Mortensen as Aragorn  
- Orlando Bloom as Legolas  
- Sean Astin as Samwise Gamgee  
- Ian Holm as Bilbo Baggins  
- Christopher Lee as Saruman  

**Duration**: 3h 48m  
**Rating**: PG-13
