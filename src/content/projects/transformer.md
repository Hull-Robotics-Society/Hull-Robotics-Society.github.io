The Transformer started in January 2026. The plan is a legged robot with high-torque servos at the hips and knees, driven through a PCA9685 servo board.

A Raspberry Pi handles the high-level control and an ESP32 deals with the real-time side. An e-stop switch is part of the design from day one.
