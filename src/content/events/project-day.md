Every Wednesday afternoon in term time the Robot Lab is ours for a Project Day: time to get your hands on the robots, solder, print, code and test.

Drop in whenever suits you between 13:30 and 17:00, whether you stay for an hour or the whole afternoon. The weekly [Project Meeting](/events/weekly-meetup/) on Monday is where we plan what to work on.
