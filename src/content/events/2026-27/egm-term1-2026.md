The role of Vice President is currently vacant, so we're holding an Emergency General Meeting (EGM) to elect a new Vice President for the rest of the 2026–27 academic year.

## What the Vice President does

The Vice President of the Society is responsible for:

- Assisting the President in calling meetings, sharing agenda items and writing minutes.
- Chairing meetings when the President is unavailable.
- Booking events and rooms, and completing external speaker forms when needed.
- Making sure society communications, including social media, are appropriate.
- Keeping the President for Student Communities and the relevant Hull University Students' Union (HUSU) staff up to date with changes to the constitution form.
- Checking that there is money in the society's bank account before a committee member authorises a payment.

## Interested in standing?

If you'd like to put yourself forward, let the committee know before the meeting: email us at [uniofhullroboticsociety@gmail.com](mailto:uniofhullroboticsociety@gmail.com) or speak to us at a [Project Meeting](/events/weekly-meetup/). If you have questions about the role, ask any member of the [Executive Team](/about/#team).
