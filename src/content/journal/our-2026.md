## A Year of Innovation and Growth

2026 has been an incredible year for the Hull Robotics Society. From exciting project launches to memorable social events, we've had plenty to celebrate.

### Projects in Progress

This year we've been working on several ambitious projects:

- **Baxter Robot Development** - Continuing to expand Baxter's capabilities with new arm segments and enhanced programming
- **Dalek Project** - Recent upgrades to our mobile frame and electronics systems, with our Dalek exploring the campus and starring in plenty of photo shoots
- **OSRC Controller** - Building a sophisticated controller system with custom hardware and software
- **Pepper Robot Development** - Experimenting with and making use of Pepper's features, allowing us to undertake interesting new adventures
### Events & Screenings

We've also had great fun with our film screening series, bringing the society together for movie nights featuring classics like *The Lord of the Rings* and *Wall-E*.

### Looking Forward

As we move into the second half of 2026, we're excited about what's ahead. Stay tuned for updates on our projects and upcoming events!

*Keep building, keep learning, and keep innovating!*
