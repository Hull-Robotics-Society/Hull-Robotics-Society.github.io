# Hull University Robotics Society Refreshers Event

**Hosted by:** Hull University Students Union (HUSU)  
**Location:** To Be Confirmed  
**Time:** To Be Confirmed  

## Join Us for an Exciting Refreshers Event!

The Hull University Robotics Society is thrilled to invite you to our Refreshers event, hosted by HUSU! This is a fantastic opportunity to meet fellow robotics enthusiasts, discover exciting projects, and learn more about what we do as a society. Whether you're a seasoned roboticist or just curious about robotics, there's something for everyone!

## What to Expect

We’ll be showcasing some of our most exciting projects, including:

- **The Dalek:** A fully functional, life-sized Dalek that’s always a crowd favorite!
- **Lego Mindstorms Projects:** Hands-on robotics built with creativity and innovation.
- **CAD & 3D Printing:** Explore how we design and create custom components for our robots.
- **Arduino Projects:** See the power of microcontrollers in action.
- **SlimVR Trackers:** Dive into wearable tracking technology.
- **Open-Source Remote Controller:** Discover how we’re building universal controllers for robotics.
- **Mind-Controlled Robots:** Learn how we use EEG to control robots with brainwaves.

## Why Attend?

- **Explore Our Project Catalogue:** Get an overview of the diverse projects we’re working on and find something that sparks your interest.
- **Meet the Team:** Chat with our friendly members and see how you can get involved.
- **Hands-On Demonstrations:** Interact with our projects and see them in action.
- **Be Inspired:** Learn new skills, collaborate on innovative ideas, and be part of our growing community.

Stay tuned for updates on the exact time and location. Don’t miss out on this exciting event—we can’t wait to see you there!
