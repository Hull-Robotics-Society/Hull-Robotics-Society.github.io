## Designing the OSRC Enclosure
The OSRC has been **fully CAD-designed in-house**, ensuring a comfortable grip and ergonomic layout. Key features:

- **Compact & lightweight design**, inspired by the Steam Deck.
- **Internal space optimization** for the Raspberry Pi, Teensy, and wiring.
- **Modular design**, allowing for future hardware upgrades.

### **3D Printing Process**
- Printed using **high-strength PLA+ and PETG**.
- Modular assembly allows for easy replacements.
- Custom button caps and joystick mounts for better control.
